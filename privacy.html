Privacy policy

This policy describes how 220 BioWorX collects and uses information when you visit www.220bioworx.com or submit a research inquiry or order request.

Effective upon posting. Last updated: August 4, 2026.

Summary: We use website analytics (Google Analytics) to understand traffic. If you submit a form or checkout request, we receive the information you provide (such as name, organization, and email) to respond and process research orders. We do not operate a public retail storefront. This site is for Research Use Only materials.

1. Who we are

220 BioWorX supplies Research Use Only (RUO) biomolecular research materials to qualified research purchasers. We are based in the Fort Myers / Lee County, Florida region and ship nationwide. Contact: Customerservice@220bioworx.com.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the site, we may collect:

  • Information you submit — for example name, organization, work email, role, notes, buyer/institution type, and order or cart details when you send a research inquiry or request an invoice at checkout.
  • Technical and usage data — such as pages viewed, approximate location (city/region level), device/browser type, referral source, and similar analytics data collected automatically via cookies or similar technologies.
  • Session data on your device — for example age-gate acknowledgment and shopping cart contents stored in your browser (session storage) so the site can function.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive health information through this website. Do not submit protected health information or clinical patient data.

3. Cookies, analytics & Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC) to measure site traffic and usage. Google may set or read cookies and similar technologies and receive data such as your IP address (which may be truncated/anonymized depending on Google settings), device information, and pages visited.

Google’s processing of data is described in Google’s policies, including: Google Privacy Policy and information about Google Analytics data. You may be able to limit analytics cookies through browser settings or Google’s available opt-out tools.

We use analytics to understand which pages are visited, improve the website, and evaluate marketing or outreach effectiveness. We do not use analytics to sell your personal information as a product list to third parties.

4. How we use information

  • Respond to research inquiries and customer service requests
  • Process order / invoice requests and payment coordination
  • Operate, secure, and improve the website
  • Comply with law and enforce site policies (including RUO terms)
  • Analyze aggregate traffic and site performance

5. Sharing of information

We may share information with:

  • Service providers that help us run the site or communications (for example email/form delivery, analytics, hosting, payment-related tools you choose to use such as cryptocurrency invoice services).
  • Professional advisors or authorities when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

We do not sell personal information for money. Some analytics/ad technology providers may process data as independent controllers under their own policies.

6. Retention

We retain inquiry and order-related communications as needed for business operations, recordkeeping, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. Analytics data is retained according to our analytics tool settings and provider defaults.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to a small business website. No method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

8. Age restriction

This website is intended for users 21 years of age or older who can lawfully purchase Research Use Only materials. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your choices

  • You may browse without submitting forms (analytics may still apply as described above).
  • You may control cookies through your browser settings.
  • To request access, correction, or deletion of personal information you submitted to us, email Customerservice@220bioworx.com. We may need to verify your request and retain certain records where legally required.

Products discussed on this site are Research Use Only. Not for human or veterinary use. Not FDA approved. Not a 503A/503B compounding pharmacy. This privacy policy does not change product intended-use restrictions. See also our legal disclosures and return/refund policy.
